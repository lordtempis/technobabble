This port reimplements the launcher and widget scripts in C#. The original
share/hollywood/map.jpg is embedded unchanged. The original soundwave.mp4 was
decoded into the included wave.jpg contact sheet. The sheet holds 208 one-second
samples arranged in 16 columns of 96 x 54 pixels. The source build embeds the
sheet directly. No decoder is needed for building or playing the application.
