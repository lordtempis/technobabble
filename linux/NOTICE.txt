This Linux edition uses C# implementations of the launcher and widgets. The
upstream world map (share/hollywood/map.jpg) was resized and embedded as RGB
data. Its soundwave.mp4 was sampled into a 208-frame atlas and embedded in the
same format. The original map and sampled atlas JPEGs are included with the
source alongside the preconverted resources.
